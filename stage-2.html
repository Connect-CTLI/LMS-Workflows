Load participants and open

The LMS Admin loads your participants from your unit cohort sheet. Make sure the sheet is complete in good time.

1 to 2 weeks before the opening date
Request trackerSee where your course is›
Lead time: 1 to 2 weeks

Your cohort sheet needs to be complete at least one week before the opening date, so participants can be loaded and checked before the course opens.

What happens

  1. 1

    Complete the cohort sheet

    All participant details are captured and checked on your unit cohort sheet.

    TCUnit intern
  2. 2

    Participants uploaded

    The LMS Admin uploads participants from the cohort sheet into the course, with their course, district and school groups.

    LMS Admin
    Automatic email: You are told participants are loaded, with a link to check the list on Moodle.
  3. 3

    Check, add and correct

    Check the participant list on Moodle. Additions and corrections can still be made. Trained unit interns can correct records on Moodle themselves, but always let the LMS Admin know.

    TCUnit intern
  4. 4

    Course opens

    The LMS Admin opens the course on the opening date given in your request.

    LMS Admin
    Automatic email: You are told your course is open, with its closing date.

Unit cohort sheets

Capture participants here. The Master cohort sheet and the Moodle upload read from these sheets.
